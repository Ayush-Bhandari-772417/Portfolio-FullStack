        - There are several common issues that can arise during site migration, including the following:
            1.  Loss of data or content:
                        This can happen if the migration process is not carried out properly, leading to the loss of important data or content from the site.
            2.  Broken links and images:
                        Site migration can cause links and images on the site to break, leading to a poor user experience and potential loss of traffic and revenue.
            3.  Loss of search engine rankings:
                        If the migration is not carried out carefully, it can result in a loss of search engine rankings, leading to a decrease in organic traffic to the site.
            4.  Downtime:
                        Site migration can result in downtime, which can be frustrating for users and lead to a loss of traffic and revenue.
            5.  Compatibility issues:   
                        In some cases, the new site may not be compatible with certain browsers or devices, leading to poor user experience and potential loss of traffic.
            To avoid these issues, it is important to carefully plan and execute the site migration process, and to test the new site thoroughly before making it live.
